Research norms

Applied to every faculty member working in a Research Theme, and issued as an official CARR circular approved by the steering committee. FA-level targets are agreed with each FA Lead at handover.

Project alignmentEvery project belongs to a theme+
  • Senior Design Projects, institutional research (RFA), internships and sponsored work are each aligned to one Research Theme.
  • Student progress is tracked as theme progress.
  • It is recognised as the guiding faculty member's contribution to CARR Lab.
Conference papersEvery project publishes each semester+
  • Each project handled during the semester contributes conference papers, affiliated to CARR Lab.
  • The expectation is phased in over the coming semesters.
Journal articlesRegular submissions and acceptances+
  • Every faculty member submits journal articles each semester.
  • Acceptances are tracked over the year, since review times are outside the author's control.
Quality floorIndexed, reviewed, credible venues+
  • Scopus or Web of Science indexed venues only; no predatory journals or conferences.
  • Preference for Q1 and Q2 journals.
  • Every paper passes CARR's internal review before submission.
AffiliationOne consistent line on every paper+
Center for Automation and Robotics Research (CARR Lab), Department of <author's department>, KLE Technological University, Hubballi, India
  • The department is the one the faculty member or student belongs to.
  • Joint papers with CEVI, CIM or CAIR name both labs, using the line agreed with that lab.

Enablers

What CARR provides so the norms can be met.

Protected research block

A fixed weekly half-day for CARR research, agreed with the HoD.

Internal paper review

One reviewer from another theme reads every paper before submission.

Priority for FA Leads

First call on students and budget for their Focus Area.

Visibility and recognition

CARR Research Day each semester, the CARR Lab LinkedIn page and institutional recognition.

Immediate next steps

Setting the structure in motion.

  1. One-to-one handover with each FA Lead; FA targets agreed.
  2. Official CARR circular on norms and FA structure issued.
  3. FA kick-off meetings, jointly with CEVI, CIM and CAIR.
  4. CIM nominates its FA3 lead and single point of contact.
  5. CAIR assigns its faculty to FA4 themes.
  6. FA1 maps all Department of Automation & Robotics projects to its themes.
  7. CARR Research Tracker live with every FA's entries.
