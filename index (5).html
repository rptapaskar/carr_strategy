Overview

Three ideas hold the strategy together

CARR Lab's research is organised so that every faculty member knows where their work belongs, who they work with, and how progress will be recognised.

Clear structure

Research is grouped into Focus Areas and Research Themes. Each Focus Area has a CARR Lead and, where shared, a lead from the partner lab.

Steady rhythm

Weekly reviews in each Focus Area, fortnightly briefings to the Director and monthly KPI reviews by the steering committee keep work moving.

Visible results

Every project is aligned to a theme, papers follow shared quality norms, and all results are recorded in the CARR Research Tracker.

Governance

Who steers, who leads, who delivers

A steering committee sets direction. FA Leads run their Focus Areas. Faculty and students carry out the research in each theme.

Steering Committee

Sets direction, targets and norms

Approves the research norms, reviews KPIs monthly and backs FA Leads in their decisions. Industry relationships are held by the Director.

Research norms

Shared expectations for every faculty member

Issued as an official CARR circular approved by the steering committee. Focus Area targets are agreed with each FA Lead.

Project alignmentEvery project belongs to a theme+
  • Senior Design Projects, institutional research projects, internships and sponsored work are each aligned to one Research Theme.
  • Student progress is tracked as the theme's progress.
  • It is recognised as the guiding faculty member's contribution to CARR Lab.
Conference papersEvery project publishes each semester+
  • Each project handled during the semester contributes conference papers affiliated to CARR Lab.
  • The expectation is phased in over the coming semesters.
Journal articlesRegular submissions and acceptances+
  • Every faculty member submits journal articles each semester.
  • Acceptances are tracked across the year, since review times are outside the author's control.
QualityIndexed, reviewed, credible venues+
  • Scopus or Web of Science indexed venues only; no predatory journals or conferences.
  • Preference for Q1 and Q2 journals.
  • Every paper passes CARR's internal review before submission.
AffiliationOne consistent line on every paper+
Center for Automation and Robotics Research (CARR Lab), Department of <author's department>, KLE Technological University, Hubballi, India
  • The department is the one the faculty member or student belongs to, for example the Department of Mechanical Engineering.
  • Joint papers with CEVI, CIM or CAIR name both labs, using the line agreed with that lab.

Enablers

What CARR provides so the norms can be met

Protected research block

A fixed weekly half-day for CARR research, agreed with the Head of Department.

Internal paper review

A reviewer from another theme reads every paper for venue fit, novelty, writing and affiliation before it is submitted.

Priority for FA Leads

FA Leads receive priority for students and budget in their Focus Area.

Visibility and recognition

A CARR Research Day each semester, the CARR Lab LinkedIn page and recognition within KLE Tech and beyond.

Research infrastructure

Facilities the Focus Areas build on

CoFACT floor

A warehouse-like testbed for AMR navigation and fleet research, shared with CIM.

Motion capture

OptiTrack system for precise ground truth of AMR motion on the CoFACT floor.

AMR fleet

ROS 2 mobile robots and an industrial AMR platform for multi-robot work.

Collaborative robots and arms

Industrial cobots and open-source research arms for manipulation and policy learning.

Condition-monitoring testbeds

A rotating-equipment rig with vibration sensing, and industry pilot setups.

HRC Cell

A human–robot collaboration cell, being built for collaborative research.
